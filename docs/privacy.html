Privacy policy

Fit the Queue (formerly Playlist Lens), version 2.0.0. Last updated 2026-11-20.

Fit the Queue is a browser extension that runs on www.youtube.com. It has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no ads.

What it saves

In your browser's extension storage: your time budget, hide-watched setting, sort, playback speed, how a budget packs videos, study-plan defaults, whether you dismissed the support cards, a count of how often you used a budget or sort, and the date you first used it. If progress memory is on, it also keeps a local snapshot for each playlist you open: the playlist id and title, counts, percent watched, time left, and when it was saved. Those snapshots are not synced. Title and channel filters are not saved. Uninstalling the extension deletes the local copy. The full policy is PRIVACY.md.

What it reads

The playlist page you have open, so it can read each video's title, channel, length, views, date, and watched bar. On Watch Later, a removal you confirm uses YouTube's own Remove from Watch Later button.

What it sends

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. Export files are created on your device when you click the button. The Ko-fi and store rating links open only when you click them.
